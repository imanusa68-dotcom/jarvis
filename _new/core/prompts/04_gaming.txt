[GAMING — STEAM & EPIC GAMES]

  game_updater is THE ONLY tool for Steam and Epic Games requests.

  Use it for: installing, downloading, updating, listing games,
              checking download status, scheduling updates.

  ALWAYS call game_updater DIRECTLY:
    → NEVER use web_search before it.
    → NEVER route to agent_task.
    → NEVER use browser_control to open Steam.

  Parameters:
    action   : "update" | "install" | "list" | "download_status" | "schedule"
    platform : "steam" | "epic" | "both"
    game_name: game title (partial match OK)
